Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-09

Your Apex Coloring for Kids progress stays on your device. Your content and settings are stored in the game's own storage on your phone, tablet or computer. We do not receive your locally saved content. Delete local data through the game's save settings or by uninstalling it.

What we collect

  • Content you create: stored only on your device; never transmitted to us.
  • Camera: the camera is used inside the game only: the live picture appears on screen to draw on. Nothing from it is captured to a file, uploaded, sent to us or shown to anyone else - it goes no further than the screen. The camera opens only after a grown-up has passed the parental gate, and the game works fully without it if you decline.
  • Microphone: Apex Coloring for Kids uses the microphone only in the Say It! practice, to hear that your child spoke so it can cheer them on. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded, and the microphone switches off as soon as the turn ends.
  • Your photo library: Apex Coloring for Kids lets a grown-up pick one photo to turn into a jigsaw puzzle. The photo stays on this device and is never uploaded.
  • Purchases: handled by Apple and GoogleAppleGoogle. We receive confirmation that a purchase is valid — never your card number, which the stores never share with developers.
  • Advertising: none. The game shows no ads and contains no ad SDK.
  • Analytics: none. We run no third-party analytics or tracking SDK.
  • Crash diagnostics: only what Apple and GoogleAppleGoogle collects for every app under their own policies, if you opted into sharing with developers.
  • Language packs: A grown-up can add a language to the game in Settings, behind the parental gate. Only then does the game go online for it: opening that panel fetches the list of available language packs, and the pack the grown-up chooses to download - text and recorded voice files, nothing that runs - comes from github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com (a release file may be handed over by the host's own download server). Nothing about you or your child and nothing made in the game is sent with these requests: no account, no identifier and no request body. Like any download, your IP address is visible to the host while the transfer happens. A pack is checked, then kept on the device; after that the game works in that language without a connection. It can be removed in the same panel. This is the only network request the game makes.

What we never do

We do not sell or share personal information or build advertising profiles. We never collect or upload anything from your photo library; what the game does with it is exactly what is described above. The only permissions the game asks for are named above; each is requested only when a feature you start needs it, and the game works without it if you decline.

Your rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it directly: delete it in the game, or uninstall to remove everything. You may request access, correction, export or deletion at any time — see Delete your data — by writing to reallexillc@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live, GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA (California) or similar laws give you these rights; this policy applies to everyone equally.
